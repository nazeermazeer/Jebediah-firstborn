# Jebediah-firstborn

**Jebediah Kerman Jr.** (or "Jeb Jr.") would be the ultimate product of a Kerbal who views a massive liquid-fuel explosion not as a failure, but as a "highly energetic staging event."

Here is how the lore of Jeb's firstborn son would unfold:

### 🚀 The Infancy of Jeb Jr.
• **The First Toy:** While other Kerbal toddlers 
play with wooden blocks, Jeb Jr's crib is rigged with functional, miniature Sepratrons. His mobile consists of rotating, un-strutted booster rockets.

• **First Words:** Instead of "Mama" or "Papa," his first word is a joyfully screamed **"MORE BOOSTERS!"**

• **The Stroller:** Jeb custom-builds his son's stroller out of a Mk1 Command Pod, a heat shield, and three parachutes (which Jeb regularly forgets to arm).
